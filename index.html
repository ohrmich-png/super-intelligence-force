Explainer

What is the Super Intelligence Force?

On the morning of October 4, 2026, Donald Trump announced on Truth Social that he was forming the Super Intelligence Force (SIF) — a White House task force to coordinate the federal government's entire approach to artificial intelligence, which he now insists be called "Super Intelligence" (SI).

The Force is led by Jay Clayton, the Director of National Intelligence, effectively making him the administration's new AI — sorry, SI — czar. It reports directly to Trump and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles.

"The Super Intelligence Force is tasked with coordinating the effort of the Federal Government to ensure that America continues to lead the World in Super Intelligence, which many say is bigger than the Industrial Revolution, and the Internet, and will protect the interests, and improve the lives, of all Americans." — Donald J. Trump, Truth Social, Oct 4, 2026

The announcement caps a week of rebranding: on September 29, Trump signed the executive order "Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence," directing every executive-branch agency to replace "AI" with "SI" in official communications. Days earlier, top AI companies signed the voluntary White House Accord on Super Intelligence at a White House meeting.

The lighter side

Let's talk about the name

We're keeping this section light, because the policy is serious and the branding is… a choice.

  • It was focus-grouped. Before the executive order, Trump polled followers on X and Truth Social on alternatives — "superior intelligence" and "supreme intelligence" were in the running. "Super Intelligence" won.
  • "No more AI. SI, it's better." — Elon Musk, October 4, confirming on X that SpaceXAI will become SpaceXSI. The unit was only created in July 2026, when SpaceX acquired xAI — so this is its second identity change in about three months.
  • The pledge had a typo. TechCrunch noted the White House's voluntary AI safety pledge — signed by the industry's top executives — "included an impressive typo." Off to a super-intelligent start.
  • Critics are having a field day. As BBN Times put it: "The rebranding has drawn mockery from critics." Meanwhile the order gives White House officials 60 days to draft statutory language formally defining "Super Intelligence" — so Congress may soon debate the legal meaning of a meme.

Honest framing: the name is funny, but the 120-day report could shape U.S. AI policy for years. That's why this page exists.
